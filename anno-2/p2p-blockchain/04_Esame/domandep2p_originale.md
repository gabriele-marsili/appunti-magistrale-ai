# Domande P2P
In generale fa domande abbastanza ampie e non si sofferma su piccoli dettagli. Fa una domanda su un argomento grosso e importante e ti lascia fare un discorso in cui puoi dire perché è necessario un sistema, quali sono le sue caratteristiche ecc. Ad esempio, è stato importante negli orali di ricordare tutte e tre le caratteristiche dell'hashing crittografico.
Fa domande per indirizzarti a ciò che vuole sapere molto tranquillamente.
Oggi non ha fatto domande su Ethereum e su solidity perché hanno fatto tutti il progetto. Sugli ultimi argomenti, applicazioni della blockchain e NFT non ha fatto domande, ma non ci sono garanzie che non le faccia a chi ha fatto il progetto su Bitcoin.
Considera gli argomenti grossi Ethereum, Bitcoin, Strutture Dati e Metodi crittografici, Attacchi.

In general she does ample questions and doesn't fixate on small details. She will ask a question on a big and important argument and lets you discuss why a system is necessary, what are its characteristics etc.  E.g. it was important in oral exams to remember the three characteristics of cryptographic hashing and the characteristics of consistent hashing.
She will ask questions to lead you to discuss what she wants you to know. Usually she asks less questions on the last topics, such as blockchain applications and tokens, but there is no guaranties, expecially since most of the exams people chose to do the programming project.
The "big topics" are Ethereum, Bitcoin, Data structures and cryptographic methods, attacks.

### Structure of the exam
The first twenty minutes or so are dedicated either to the project or to the presentation. For the project, she will ask to demonstrate how it works, comment on the quality of the code and the implementation choices, sometime ask to explain why something was done the way it was.
For the presentation, she will let you talk asking questions about the concepts included in the program. Example, when I nominated malleability attacks she asked me to explain more in detail how they work. So if you do the Bitcoin project it's still better to know Bitcoin in detail.
She's very nice and calm.
